The Straight Talk

Are your used tires safe?

100%. We wouldn't sell a tire we wouldn't put on our own cars. Every used tire is air-pressure tested and visually inspected for sidewall integrity and tread depth.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are always welcome, but an appointment guarantees you a slot on the rack and gets you in and out faster.

Can you install lift kits?

Yes. We specialize in truck leveling kits and full suspension lifts. We also provide wheel and tire packages that complement your new ride height.

Do you offer financing?

We have several flexible payment options available to help you get the tires or wheels you need today. Ask us for details.

How long does an alignment take?

Typically about 45 minutes to an hour. We use professional-grade alignment machines to ensure your specs are spot on.
