Common Questions

How long will my repair take? expand_more
Most maintenance and minor repairs are completed same-day. Complex diagnostics or transmission work may take 1-3 days depending on parts availability. Alex will always give you a firm timeline before starting.
Do I need an appointment for RI inspection? expand_more
While we accept walk-ins, we highly recommend calling ahead to schedule a specific time to ensure minimal wait for your RI State Safety and Emissions inspection.
Are your prices competitive with dealerships? expand_more
Usually, we are 20-40% more affordable than a dealership. We don't have the massive overhead of a showroom and we only recommend the repairs your car actually needs to be safe and reliable.
Do you work on foreign cars? expand_more
Yes. Alex has extensive experience with both domestic (Ford, GM, Dodge) and import (Toyota, Honda, Subaru, VW, BMW) vehicles. We use manufacturer-specific diagnostics to ensure accurate repair.
