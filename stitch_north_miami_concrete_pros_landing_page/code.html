Local FAQ

How does Miami heat affect concrete? expand_more
High heat can cause concrete to dry too quickly, leading to cracks. We use specialized retarder admixtures and curing compounds designed for South Florida's climate to ensure a slow, strong cure.
Do I need a permit for a new driveway? expand_more
Yes, most municipalities in North Miami require a permit for concrete work. We handle the entire permitting process for you, ensuring everything meets local building codes.
How long before I can drive on it? expand_more
You can walk on it after 24 hours. For vehicles, we recommend waiting 7 days for the concrete to reach about 70% of its structural strength.
Do you offer stamped concrete patterns? expand_more
Yes, we have over 20 patterns ranging from slate and cobblestone to wood plank finishes, with various color pigments to match your home.
