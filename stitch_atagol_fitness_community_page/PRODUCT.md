# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Women in Pacific Grove, CA, primarily 40s–60s. Locals who have either never found a fitness home that felt right, or who've walked away from big-box gyms feeling unseen. They're not training for Instagram — they're training for their actual lives: bone density, energy, confidence, friendship. They want to be coached, not processed.

## Product Purpose

Convert page visitors into booked free consultations. The page needs to communicate that Atagol Fitness is a small, intimate, women-only coaching studio that takes each member seriously — not a gym, a community. Success is a visitor who picks up the phone or submits the form because the page made them feel, for the first time, like this place was built for someone exactly like them.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, expert, neighborly. Pacific Grove earned its character through the Monarch Butterfly Sanctuary and Victorian cottages — unhurried, natural, dignified. Atagol carries that same unpretentious confidence. ATAGOL = All Things Are Gifts Of Life: movement as gratitude, not punishment.

## Anti-references

- Big-gym corporate (Planet Fitness, LA Fitness energy — loud, masculine, mirror-selfie)
- Wellness-beige influencer (soft sage, cream bg, script fonts, "nourish your soul" Instagram cleanliness)
- Clinical medical spa (white/navy/sans — looks like a dermatology practice)
- SaaS fitness app (Fitbit/Peloton dark-mode neon aesthetic)
- Generic soft-landing-page AI output: cream body bg, Playfair Display, rounded icon circles above every card, identical 3/4-column card grids, eyebrow labels above every section heading

## Design Principles

1. **Belonging over branding.** Every visual and copy decision asks: does this make someone feel seen, or does it make the brand feel impressive? The answer is always belonging first.
2. **Structured confidence.** Sharp-ish corners, real weight in the type, a dominant color that doesn't apologize. Not soft, not clinical — grounded.
3. **Pacific Grove, not generic coastal.** The forest green of Monterey cypress and coastal parks, not the beige of a wellness retreat. Color and texture come from the actual place.
4. **Editorial, not templated.** Programs read like a menu, not a feature card grid. Testimonials have hierarchy, not identical boxes. Each section earns its layout from its content.
5. **Julie is the product.** The studio's credibility runs through the founder. Surface the human, not the brand system.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum throughout. All body text ≥4.5:1 contrast. Large text (18px+ or 14px bold) ≥3:1. Prefers-reduced-motion honored — all scroll animations fall back to instant transitions. Form labels always visible (no placeholder-as-label). Semantic heading hierarchy.
