Meet Julie & the Atagol story

ATAGOL stands for **All Things Are Gifts Of Life**. Founded by Julie, Atagol Fitness was born from a desire to create a sanctuary where women could reclaim their strength without the typical gym intimidation.

Our mission is simple: to provide high-level coaching in an environment that feels like home. We believe that movement is a gift, and our role is to help you unwrap it safely, effectively, and with joy.

Common questions

I haven't exercised in years. Is Atagol right for me? expand_more
Absolutely. Most of our members started right where you are. Our small group format allows us to scale every single exercise to your current fitness level, ensuring you're safe and successful from day one.
What exactly is Bone Builders? expand_more
Bone Builders is a specialized resistance training program designed to combat osteopenia and osteoporosis. We focus on progressive loading and weight-bearing exercises that stimulate bone growth and improve overall stability.
How many people are in a group? expand_more
We keep our groups intentionally small, typically between 4 to 6 women. This ensures you get personalized attention from the coach while still enjoying the energy of a supportive community.
Do I need to bring my own equipment? expand_more
Nope! Our studio is fully equipped with everything you need. Just bring a water bottle, comfortable workout clothes, and your sneakers.
