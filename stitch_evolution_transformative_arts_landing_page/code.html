Our Philosophy

More than
a studio.
A calling.

This is a calling steeped in passion and purpose. We show up day in and day out — to nourish, to inspire, and to cultivate a healthier community through healing and artistic expression.

01
Connection

Personal wellbeing is inextricably linked to our environment and community. When one thrives, the other follows. We practice in ways that honor that feedback loop.

02
Communication

The body speaks truths the mind hasn't yet articulated. Movement is language — we teach you to listen, then to respond with intention and grace.

03
Collaboration

We co-create alongside artists, healers, and community activists. Health is a collective practice — we are nourished by the intelligence of the group.

04
Creativity

Whether you dance, stretch, receive a healing touch, or simply breathe — you are the artist of your own body. Movement is art-making, at any age or stage of life.

Jennifer Haydu — Founder

Teacher, healer,
lifelong student

Jennifer has spent over three decades at the intersection of movement, healing, and community. She earned national certifications as a personal trainer, medical exercise specialist, and Pilates and yoga instructor — building a practice that weaves together the dance world, Thai Yoga Massage, Esalen bodywork, and deep compassionate presence.

She completed the Yoga Therapy training program at the Niroga Institute in Oakland and channels those lessons through community outreach to schools, non-profits, and underserved populations across Monterey. Her specialty is meeting students where they are, at any age, and helping them access their own sense of wellbeing and grace.
